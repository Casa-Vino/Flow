# Flow

Calm, sensory water-play games, made for and inspired by my son, Robin.

**Play it:** https://casa-vino.github.io/Flow/

There are no timers, scores or ways to fail. Sounds are soft and fade in and out. Everything works with big picture buttons, so no reading is needed.

## Games

### Bubble Car Wash (`car-wash.html`)

Ride through a car wash from the driver's seat of a red car:

1. Water spray
2. Coloured foam (pink, blue, yellow)
3. Spinning brushes
4. Rinse
5. Dryers
6. Out into the sunshine

- The picture buttons jump to any part of the wash.
- 🔁 stays on the current part for as long as you like.
- 🔊 turns the sound on or off.
- Touching the windscreen adds foam, water drops or sparkles, depending on the stage.
- Tapping the middle of the steering wheel sounds a soft horn.

### Lagoon (`lagoon.html`)

Look straight down into clear, shallow water over sand, shells and starfish.

- Touch the water to make ripples. Drag a finger to draw wakes, and use several fingers at once.
- Ripples bend the light on the sand below and bounce off the edges.
- Little fish swim around and dart away from your finger.
- Leaves and frangipani flowers float and bob on the ripples.
- Soft lapping water, with a gentle plop when you touch and a swish when you drag.
